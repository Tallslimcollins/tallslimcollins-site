Living in the Antique Shop Showroom at 1707 Madison

In 1984 we moved to Memphis, and by fifth grade our home was literally inside my mother’s antique shop showroom at 1707 Madison Avenue—riding home in a cargo van packed to the roof with French antiques, sleeping on the wooden base of a Louis XVI armoire, and stepping out the front door into a Midtown ruled by Dancing Jimmy, the Antenna Club, and Decadence Manor across the street.
